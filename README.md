# AI Compute Faculty Development Initiative — 2026

**Personal academic learning portfolio | Portefeuille académique personnel**

Dr. Mohamed Mahmoud EL BENANY — Faculty of Sciences and Techniques, University of Nouakchott, Mauritania.

This independent repository documents learning activities, original notes, exercises and outcomes related to participation in the second cohort of the Global AI Compute Faculty Development Initiative (October 2026, Beijing). It is **not an official repository** of BAAI, FlagOS, UNDP or partner institutions.

## Topics
- AI computing fundamentals and AI system software
- High-performance AI operators, attention and operator fusion
- Distributed training and inference
- Edge AI and deployment
- Triton kernels, RMSNorm, benchmarking and performance engineering
